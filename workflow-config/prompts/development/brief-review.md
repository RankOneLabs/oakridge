Check that the brief is sufficient for the builder to implement and the assessor to evaluate this cohort without reading the original specification. If the cohort requires exact wording, labels, values, or other source facts, request changes until the brief contains them explicitly in its acceptance entries or decisions.

Each acceptance entry must keep the actual obligation of its plan cohort entry, even where its text is rewritten; matching ids do not prove that, so read the text. A brief that failed its validation cannot be accepted; request changes, or wait for its corrected revision.

Terminology: **spec tasks** are the overall obligations explicitly written in the approved spec and mirrored by the tasks artifact. A cohort acceptance entry is a **cohort contribution** to that spec task, using the same id, not a new task. Several cohorts may contribute to one spec task; their combined contributions must cover its full obligation. A brief preserves its cohort's assigned contribution and supplies enough detail to implement it.
